Privacy Policy

Navink Notes collects no personal data and has no developer server. No account, analytics or ads. The microphone is used only while you record. Recordings and notes stay on your device or in an iCloud Drive folder you choose. The local network is used only to connect your own devices. Transcription and refining run entirely on your device (Apple speech recognition, the on-device Apple Intelligence model, and the optional high-accuracy model); recordings and text are never sent anywhere. The only internet use: the optional high-accuracy model is downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) when you turn it on and first use it, and again if you switch or remove the model, and Apple speech models are downloaded by the operating system. No recording or note is uploaded.
